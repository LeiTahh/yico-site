Restore a purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Google Play or App Store account — not to the device. Reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone does not charge you again.

  1. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same store account you bought with.
  2. Open the app and go to Settings → Pro.
  3. Tap Restore purchases and wait for the store to answer.

If the store is unreachable (offline, or the store service is down), the app keeps the unlock it already has on disk and tries again later. Nothing is lost.

Still locked after restoring?

Usually one of these three.

1. A different store account than the one that paid.
2. The purchase is still pending at the store (bank authorisation).
3. You are on a build installed from outside the store.

Write to us with the app name and the store order number; we will check the same records the store shows us — we never see your card or address.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We never receive your payment details, and we cannot issue a refund ourselves — only the store can.

Google Play: request a refund  ·  App Store: report a problem

付款由 Google Play 或 App Store 处理，我们不接触支付信息，也无法自行退款；退款只能由商店发起。

Your data

All app data — recipes, wheel builds, cut lists, settings — is stored on your device only. There is no account and no server of ours. Uninstalling the app removes that data; there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Removing and reinstalling the app does not remove a Pro purchase: the store still remembers it.

所有数据只在本机，没有账号、也没有我们的服务器。卸载即删除；我们这边没有可删的东西。 卸载重装不影响已购买的 Pro，商店仍然记得这笔购买。
