Yico Photo Frame

Turn your photo album into a frame
that never stops playing.

Yico Photo Frame turns an Android phone or tablet into a full-screen digital photo frame — your own photos, your own music, no account.

把闲置的 Android 手机或平板变成一台全屏电子相框：用自己的照片、自己的音乐，不用注册账号。

Yico Photo Frame

  • Full-screen slideshow straight from your local albums
  • Clock, date and weather overlaid on the photo
  • Background music from your own library
  • Screensaver mode — keeps running while the device is idle
  • Folder, hidden-photo and shuffle support
  • No account · No ads · No watermark

Availability

Android · distributed through Google Play

Yico Photo Frame is published on Google Play. Every core feature is free. Photos stay on your device — nothing is uploaded.
